# NOTES

## Design decisions (2026-10-27)
- **The idea:** the printed lineup card is the main design device. Batting order, rotation, bullpen and bench live on one card with a hard offset shadow; it is the only element with a shadow.
- **Style reference translation:** PuckPedia / CapFriendly / Baseball Savant became dense tables, tabular numbers, one payroll bar, and plain labels. No marketing sections.
- **Palette (round 5):** ground #F2F5F7, ink #16212E, teal #19697A (actions, links, selected, "good" data), crimson #A3162D (identity rules, over the tax line, errors, "bad" data), aqua #E4EFF1 for panels, header navy #10243A. Light theme. The earlier green-and-chalk palette was replaced.
- **Type (round 7):** Hanken Grotesk throughout, in weights 400 to 700, with tabular figures so numbers line up. Headings are sentence case at weight 600. Five sizes (13 / 16 / 24 / 40 / 64) unchanged.
- **Playfulness 2, motion none (both set in your brief).** No animations; only state feedback.
- **Corners (round 7):** softly rounded (6 / 10 / 14 px) on buttons, inputs, panels, cards, tags and bars; previously square. Shadows: only the lineup card has a soft one.
- **Stack:** plain HTML, CSS and JS. No build step.
- **Auto choices:** name "Baseball Barometer" (rename in `shell()` in `app.js`; first named "Open Front Office"), colors, fonts, five seasons (2027 to 2031), six fictional teams.

## Known limits
- Demo data only; real data not connected (see README).
- Forum, profile and home feed are single-browser, not shared.
- Model starting weights are placeholders.
- Contracts are flat (same salary each year). No arbitration, qualifying offers, service time or Rule 5 yet.

## Changelog
- 2026-10-27 (round 8, player lists): The player tables on GM Mode (40-man, Farm, Free agents, Trade) and Front Office (model ranking, contract results) are now grouped "player strips" instead of tables. Ideas borrowed from the MLB.com team roster (players grouped under position sections) and the Baseball Reference player header (a strip of labelled stat blocks); the look is our own: a round position badge in place of a photo (teal tint for hitters, navy tint for pitchers), name with an age and contract line, labelled stat blocks with tinted WAR chips, and actions underneath. Trade strips are clickable and tinted when picked. Contract results show a small bar chart of WAR by year. All values, buttons and drag-to-lineup behavior are unchanged; the old table CSS was removed. Note: the forum and stats searches are text boxes, unchanged; the site has no player search box.
- 2026-10-27 (round 7, less blocky): Same layout, softer look. One font family (Hanken Grotesk) replaces the condensed/mono mix; headings are lighter sentence case with no caps-and-rule titles; table heads are quiet labels over thin lines, with soft row dividers instead of stripes and dark header bars; dotted rules became soft solid lines; corners are gently rounded; the lineup card lost its heavy border and hard offset shadow; the header stripe is 2px; message boxes lost their thick left bar. Colors unchanged. Two fixes to keep desktop tables as they were: 2px less side padding in the GM tables, and column headings may wrap.
- 2026-10-27 (round 6, rename): Site renamed from "Open Front Office" to "Baseball Barometer" (header brand, all six page titles and share titles, README, notes). Home headline changed from "Run a front office." to "An Armchair GM's Dream"; the text under it is unchanged. The Front Office page, its menu link, the folder name `open-front-office` and the internal save key are unchanged (renaming the save key would erase saved data).
- 2026-10-27 (round 5, restyle): New colors and text formatting, inspired by Baseball Savant (light aqua info panels, condensed headlines, monospaced numbers), Baseball Reference (crimson rules on light surfaces) and PuckPedia (navy header, dense right-aligned tables, smaller "M" after dollar amounts, underline tabs). Own choices: crimson-underlined tabs, teal offset shadow on the lineup card, and colored WAR/surplus cells where teal means better and crimson means worse (the reverse of Savant's convention, so crimson means "costly or bad" everywhere on this site). Layout, features and data untouched. Body font changed from Public Sans to Source Sans 3 during testing because the wider font clipped table cells and lineup names.
