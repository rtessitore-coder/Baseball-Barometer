# Baseball Barometer

A free baseball roster-building site: GM Mode (lineup card, free agents, trades, five seasons ahead), a Front Office page for your own projection model, a forum, a profile, and a stat glossary. No paywall, no messaging.

## How to open it
Double-click `index.html`. It needs no install and no build step. (Fonts load from Google Fonts, so they need an internet connection; the site still works without them, just in a fallback font.)

## What is demo and what is real
**Everything in the player data is demo.** The six teams, every player, contract, stat and free-agent class are generated by `data.js` and are fictional. They are labeled as demo on every page. The forum's three starter posts are also fictional demos.

**What is real:**
- The stat glossary definitions and formulas (standard published definitions; check them against the [MLB glossary](https://www.mlb.com/glossary)).
- The mechanics: 26-man and 40-man limits, options, free agency, the payroll-vs-tax-line bar, trades with a simple acceptance rule, the projection model, and the CSV import.

**Demo rules to know about** (so you can defend them):
- The "tax line" ($200M) is a fictional number you can change on the GM page. It is not the real competitive balance tax.
- A trade is accepted if the other team gets at least 5% more "value" than it gives. Value is projected WAR, or 60% of a prospect's demo ceiling.
- Positions are not checked on the lineup card.
- Model starting weights are placeholders, not fitted to data.
- Saving: everything (moves, posts, model) is stored in this browser only (`localStorage`). The forum is therefore not public. Clearing site data erases it; My Profile has an "Erase my data" button to reset on purpose.

## Contract value model (modeled on your "MLB Contract Model" sheet)
Front Office has a contract model with the same structure as your sheet: a player's career, last-season and season-before WAR, age at signing, contract years and dollars go in; a baseline WAR, WAR for each contract year, total WAR, value, surplus and an OVERPAY/UNDERPAY verdict come out.

How this version calculates it (every number is a setting the user controls):
- **Baseline WAR** = weighted average of career / last season / season before, using the user's weights.
- **WAR in each contract year** = baseline minus the user's yearly decline for each year of age so far, never below 0. The decline has three settings: age 30 and under, 31 to 33, 34 and up.
- **Value** = total contract WAR x dollars per WAR. **Surplus** = value minus contract dollars.

**This does not reproduce your sheet.** The CSV contains values, not formulas, so I could not recover how the sheet computes baseline WAR, the age curve, ROI or Surplus Value. The starting settings are my placeholders. With them, all three players in your sample file come out OVERPAY, while your sheet says Suarez is underpaid. Once the sheet's rules are known, the settings or the formula can be changed to match.

Import: a CSV with columns for player, career WAR, 2025 WAR, 2024, age at signing, contract length and contract value (the first table in your sheet) loads straight in. Its "2025" and "2024" columns become "last season" and "season before". The importer does not verify any numbers; check them against your WAR source.

"Use this model in GM Mode" adds a contract-surplus column to the 40-man and free-agent tables, using each demo player's demo WAR as the baseline.

## Making it real (not built yet)
You asked for real Baseball Reference and Baseball Savant data. That was not built, and I did not verify that it can be:
- Baseball Savant offers CSV downloads from its leaderboards. Those could be downloaded by hand and loaded into `data.js`-style data, or imported through the CSV page once it supports more than projections.
- Baseball Reference has terms about automated access. Read them before pulling anything automatically.
- A web page opened from your laptop usually cannot call those sites directly (browsers block it), so live syncing needs a small backend.
- Real rosters and contracts need a source you have checked. Do not type them from memory.
